Job Title: English–Turkish Translator

Job Summary:
We are looking for an English–Turkish Translator who can translate Turkish conversations and documents into English in a clear and accurate way, including for people who do not understand Turkish.

Must-Have Requirements:

* Good command of both English and Turkish.
* Experience in translating Turkish conversations or documents.

Preferred Requirements:

* Bachelor’s degree in Translation or a related field.
* Good communication and conversation skills.
* Strong problem-solving skills.
* Ability to complete translations quickly while maintaining accuracy.
